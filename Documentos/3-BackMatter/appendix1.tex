\chapter{Physical \texorpdfstring{\gls{FMS}}{FMS} Controller Specifications and \texorpdfstring{\gls{IO}}{I/O} Pin Mapping}
\label{app:fms_io_mapping}

This appendix presents the hardware specifications and \gls{IO} pin mapping for the Industrial Shields M-DUINO 57R+ \gls{PLC} used in the \gls{FMS} testbed.

\section{M-DUINO 57R+ Controller Technical Specifications}

\Cref{tab:mduino_57r_specs} summarizes the main hardware specifications and operating parameters of the M-DUINO 57R+ controller.

\begin{table}[htbp]
    \centering
    \caption{Hardware specifications of the Industrial Shields M-DUINO 57R+ \gls{PLC} controller.}
    \label{tab:mduino_57r_specs}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l X@{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Specification Parameter} & \textbf{Value / Feature} \\
        \midrule
        Base Microcontroller Board & Arduino Mega 2560 (ATmega2560 @ 16\,MHz) \\
        Memory (Flash / SRAM / EEPROM) & 256\,KB (8\,KB bootloader) / 8\,KB / 4\,KB \\
        Operating Input Voltage & 12 to 24\,V~DC (Rated: 24\,V~DC, Fuse protection: 2.5\,A) \\
        Max Rated Power / Max Current & 10.20\,W / 1.5\,A \\
        Integrated Network Interfaces & W5500 Ethernet, RS-485, RS-232, SPI, I2C, Serial TTL, USB 2.0 \\
        Storage Expansion & MicroSD card slot ($\mu$SD), Real-Time Clock (RTC) \\
        Total \gls{IO} Capacity & 18 Inputs / 31 Outputs (49 channels total) \\
        Digital Optoisolated Inputs & 6 channels (supporting hardware interrupts) \\
        Analog / Digital Inputs & 12 channels (0 to 10\,V~DC / 0 to 24\,V~DC, 10-bit ADC) \\
        Optoisolated Relay Outputs & 23 channels (24\,V~DC @ 2\,A / 230\,V~AC @ 4\,A max) \\
        Analog / Digital / PWM Outputs & 8 channels (0 to 10\,V~DC / 0 to 24\,V~DC, 8-bit PWM) \\
        Operating Temperature / Humidity & 0\,\text{\textdegree}C to 60\,\text{\textdegree}C / 10\% to 90\% (non-condensing) \\
        Enclosure Dimensions & 119.5\,\text{mm} $\times$ 119.3\,\text{mm} $\times$ 101\,\text{mm} (Weight: 598\,\text{g}) \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
